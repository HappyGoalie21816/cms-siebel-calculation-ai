OGM = (daily liability) * no of days / no of periods

daily liability rounded off 2 decimal places

OGM Stands for Ongoing maintanence charge
Daily liability is nothing but the amount that NRP is liable to pay every day for this current effective financial year
no of days is days from effective date of the service request calculation happened  till annual review date of the financial cycle
no of periods is nothing but periods left till annual review date either monthly or weekly ( opted by the NRP) 
these details are found in the calculation applet

Total OGM needs to be rounded off to 2 decimal points
